Typo Town privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Typo Town is a word puzzle game made by Oddly Clever. This page explains what the game keeps and what it shares.

The short version

  • Typo Town collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no servers of ours. The game doesn't use the internet at all.
  • Your progress stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, Typo Town saves two small files in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: which levels you have won and your stars, how each level went (moves, hints, undos and time taken, which the game uses for its stars and its results screen), your Typo Zoo stickers, claimed postcards, daily puzzle results and streak, and the hen outfit you picked.
  • Your settings: sound effects, music and vibration on or off.

These files never leave your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are not included in device backups.

Android permissions

  • Vibration: for small taps you can feel while playing. It is off until you turn it on.

The game does not ask for internet access, your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Sharing your daily result

After a daily puzzle, the COPY button copies a few lines of text about your result to your clipboard. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

Links out of the game

The PRIVACY POLICY button opens this page in your web browser. That is the only link in the game; your browser and this website's host (GitHub Pages) handle that visit under their own policies.

Children

Typo Town is made for players of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for a child to share with us. There is no chat, no account and no way to share personal information inside the game.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your Android settings. That deletes everything the game has stored. In the game, NEW PLAYER on the map also clears your progress.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, for example by adding ads, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
